# Whitfield Inventory Adjustment SOP

## Purpose

Correct stock discrepancies through an accountable, auditable adjustment process.

## Applies To

OWNER and MANAGER only.

## Procedure

1. Verify the inventory record, warehouse, and discrepancy before changing stock.
2. Enter a delta-based adjustment and a clear operational reason.
3. Review the before value, change amount, and resulting after value.
4. Confirm the adjustment is recorded with the actor and timestamp in the audit trail.

## Important Rules

- Never directly edit raw inventory values.
- A reason is required for every adjustment.
- Adjustments must stay within the authorized warehouse scope.
- Use receiving and fulfillment workflows for normal stock movement rather than an adjustment.

## Escalation And Restrictions

RECEIVING_STAFF and FULFILLMENT_STAFF cannot make inventory adjustments. Escalate needed corrections to a MANAGER or OWNER.
