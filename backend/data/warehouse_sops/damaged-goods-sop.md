# Whitfield Damaged Goods SOP

## Purpose

Keep damaged units separate from sellable inventory and maintain an accurate operational record.

## Applies To

All warehouse staff handling inbound or stored inventory.

## Procedure

1. Record damaged quantity separately when receiving a shipment.
2. Keep damaged units physically isolated from normal sellable stock.
3. Confirm that damaged units are not treated as available inventory for fulfillment.
4. Document any quantity or condition discrepancy through the normal receipt or inventory audit trail.
5. Escalate material discrepancies to a MANAGER for review.

## Important Rules

- Do not count damaged units as sellable or available stock.
- Do not manually edit inventory values to hide a discrepancy.
- Use controlled inventory adjustment only when an OWNER or MANAGER approves it with a reason.

## Escalation And Restrictions

RECEIVING_STAFF and FULFILLMENT_STAFF must report discrepancies. Only OWNER or MANAGER can make a controlled inventory adjustment.
