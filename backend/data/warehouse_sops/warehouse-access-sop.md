# Whitfield Warehouse Access SOP

## Purpose

Apply the Whitfield role and warehouse-scope model consistently across warehouse work.

## Applies To

All Whitfield WMS users.

## Procedure

1. Sign in with an active Whitfield account.
2. Work only within the warehouses assigned to the account.
3. Use the correct operational workflow for receiving, fulfillment, inventory review, or management.
4. Report access issues rather than attempting to bypass role or warehouse restrictions.

## Important Rules

- OWNER has broad access across warehouses.
- MANAGER has operational management access within assigned warehouse scope.
- RECEIVING_STAFF handles inbound receiving within assigned warehouse scope.
- FULFILLMENT_STAFF handles outbound fulfillment within assigned warehouse scope.
- A user interface hiding an action does not grant permission; backend authorization remains authoritative.

## Escalation And Restrictions

Only an OWNER provisions employees and assigns roles or warehouses. Do not share credentials or use another user's account.
