# Whitfield Receiving SOP

## Purpose

Process inbound shipments accurately so sellable and damaged stock are recorded separately and traceably.

## Applies To

OWNER, MANAGER, and RECEIVING_STAFF working in an authorized warehouse.

## Procedure

1. Create a receipt for the shipment and select the correct seller and an authorized warehouse.
2. Record the shipment tracking number and ticket number when supplied.
3. Scan or enter a UPC that is already registered in the product catalog.
4. Confirm the product belongs to the receipt seller before adding the item.
5. Record good quantity and damaged quantity separately. Quantities must be whole, positive units in total.
6. Review the receipt items, seller, warehouse, and quantities before completion.
7. Complete the receipt only after the shipment information is correct. Completion creates the related inventory and audit records.

## Important Rules

- Unknown UPCs are rejected. Ask an authorized manager to create the product before receiving it.
- A product belonging to another seller cannot be added to the receipt.
- Damaged quantity is not sellable inventory.
- A completed receipt cannot be modified.
- Duplicate shipment or receipt completion must not apply inventory twice.

## Escalation And Restrictions

Escalate mismatched seller, product, tracking, ticket, or quantity information to a MANAGER or OWNER. RECEIVING_STAFF may work only in assigned warehouse scope.
